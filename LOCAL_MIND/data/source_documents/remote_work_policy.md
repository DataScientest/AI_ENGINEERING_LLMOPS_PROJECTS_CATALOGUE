# Remote Work Policy — Helvetia Conseil Group

Version 3.1 — effective September 1, 2025

## 1. Purpose and scope

This policy defines the conditions for remote work for all employees of the Helvetia Conseil Group on permanent contracts or fixed-term contracts of more than six months. It replaces version 2.4 of March 1, 2023. External contractors and interns are covered by specific provisions described in their respective contracts.

## 2. General principles

Remote work is based on voluntary participation and reversibility. Each eligible employee may request up to three days of remote work per week, subject to their manager's approval and service constraints. Two on-site days per week remain mandatory, including Tuesday, defined as the common team day for the whole group.

Eligibility requires a minimum of three months of seniority, a level of autonomy deemed sufficient by the manager, and activities that are compatible with remote work. Front-desk reception and mail handling roles are not eligible.

## 3. Equipment and security

The company provides a secure laptop and a contribution of 30 euros per month toward connection costs. All processing of customer data must be done exclusively through the corporate VPN; using personal devices to access internal systems is prohibited. Printed confidential documents must not leave the premises.

In the event of a security incident (lost equipment, suspected compromise), the employee must alert the IT department within a maximum of four hours.

## 4. Work organization

Mandatory availability windows are set from 9:30 to 12:00 and from 14:00 to 17:00. Outside these windows, employees organize their time freely within the legal working time limits. The right to disconnect applies: no response is expected outside usual working hours.

Remote work from abroad is limited to ten working days per year, subject to prior approval by human resources, and only from countries of the European Union.

## 5. Monitoring and review

An annual report is presented to the employee representative bodies. The policy is reviewed every two years, or sooner in the event of a major regulatory change. Individual exemption requests are handled by human resources within fifteen working days.
